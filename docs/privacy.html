FrameBeat Privacy Policy

FrameBeat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data of any kind. The app has no accounts, no network access, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. All audio is synthesized on your device, and the drum pattern you program exists only in the app's memory for the current session — nothing is saved to disk or sent anywhere.

If you have questions about this policy, contact adamjbailey7@gmail.com.
